# CLAUDE.md

## Project

Mugathman Motors Website Redesign

## Objective

Redesign Mugathman Motors into a premium B2B commercial transport and industrial mobility website that builds trust, establishes authority, and converts qualified business inquiries.

This is **NOT** an e-commerce website. It should not function as an inventory listing platform. Instead, it should present Mugathman Motors as a trusted commercial vehicle supplier and procurement partner.

---

## Target Audience

- Logistics & Haulage Companies
- Oil & Gas / LPG Operators
- Construction & Infrastructure Companies
- Corporate & Government Procurement Teams
- Fleet Owners & Transport Entrepreneurs

---

## Brand Positioning

The website should communicate:

- Authority — Serious industry operator
- Trust — Reliable and legitimate partner
- Capability — Able to supply the right solutions
- Accessibility — Easy to contact and work with
- Scale — Established commercial business
- Specialization — Commercial transport expertise

---

## Brand Personality

- Professional
- Industrial
- Modern
- Reliable
- Confident
- Business-focused
- Premium

Avoid:

- Marketplace appearance
- Cheap or promotional language
- Generic dealership aesthetics
- Excessive animations or visual clutter

---

## Design Direction

Inspired by modern industrial brands and Tesla's product experience.

Prioritize:

- Clean layouts
- Strong typography
- Large, high-quality imagery
- Premium spacing
- Real operational photography
- Responsive design
- Fast performance

## Copywriting Guidelines

Tone:

- Clear
- Bold
- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Solution-oriented

Focus on business outcomes rather than selling products.

Use language such as:

- Commercial Vehicle Solutions
- Fleet Solutions
- Industrial Transport
- Procurement Support
- Heavy-Duty Transport
- Trusted Vehicle Brands

Avoid:

- "Best price"
- "Cheap"
- "Limited offer"
- "Buy now"

## UX Principles

Every page should answer:

- Why trust Mugathman Motors?
- What industries do they serve?
- Can they meet my business needs?
- How do I contact them?
- Why choose them over competitors?

The user journey should feel like working with a professional industrial partner, not browsing a vehicle marketplace.
